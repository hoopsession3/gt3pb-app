## FAQ

**Q: What's a animal based diet simply put?**
A: It's how humans are built to eat—centered on nutrient-rich animal foods with just enough fruit and honey to fuel performance, hormones, and recovery. It's not a fad or diet. It's a biological reset.

**Q: What's the difference between keto and carnivore?**
A: Keto is all about keeping carbs low, no matter where the food comes from. That could mean chugging seed oils and artificial sweeteners if they "fit the macros." Carnivore is strict—animal foods only, no exceptions. Animal-based finds the sweet spot: you center your meals around animal foods *but* still allow nutrient-rich, low-tox fruits and carbs that support hormones, energy, and performance. It's less rigid, more sustainable, and built for long-term vitality.

**Q: How is this better than plant based?**
A: Plant-based *sounds* clean, but most plants come with antinutrients, fake ingredients, and synthetic supplements to cover what they're missing. Animal-based gives you everything you need—complete proteins, bioavailable vitamins, essential fats—straight from the source. No supplements, vitamins needed. You absorb more, you recover faster, and you *feel* the difference.

**Q: Is it safe for kids?**
A: Not only is it safe—it's ideal. Kids need high-quality fats, proteins, and real nutrients to grow strong minds and bodies. Raw dairy, grass-fed meats, eggs, and fruit support everything from brain development to immune function
